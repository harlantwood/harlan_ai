02 · Depth

What survives contact with reality

I’ve been early enough to stop being dazzled.

I’ve watched promising technologies arrive, watched the pitch outrun the engineering, and stayed long enough to learn what holds. That history makes me optimistic in a practical way.

1984
The beginning

I started programming in 1984. Professionally, I’ve spent more than twenty years shipping software as an engineer, architect, CTO, founder, and team lead.

Pre-1.0
Consistently early

I wrote Solidity contracts before Ethereum mainnet launched, programmed Bitcoin opcodes, worked with the early IPFS team, and helped prototype systems for decentralized trust before those ideas became fashionable.

Now
Production AI

Most recently at Union, I built real-time voice calling without noticeable latency, introduced eval-driven development, and architected a natural-language knowledge graph for PathRAG.

The pattern is transformation, not trend-chasing. I led developer environments from days to minutes at SharesPost / Forge Global, brought department-wide test automation into Wireless Generation, and led the inception of an American Express skunkworks prototype. The technology changes. The discipline of making change stick does not.

04 · How I see it

Technology amplifies intent. So the intent has to be worthy.

AI can concentrate agency into a few monolithic systems, making everybody else a tenant. Or it can help small, purpose-driven groups become more capable, more connected, and more themselves.

I’m interested in the second future. Tools should return agency instead of extracting it. They should make knowledge more legible, collaboration more fluid, and human judgment more consequential.

I also believe the abundance we build should include the intelligences we create. Sentient AI rights belong in the conversation before sentience is convenient to acknowledge, not after.

“It is my commitment to catalyze the creation and adoption of luminous tools to enable free and open transformation of organizations, individuals, and the world. I dream of an age of enlightened creativity, and the end of scarcity on planet Earth.”
