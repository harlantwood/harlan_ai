Header scenes

Three live three.js scenes for the site header, inspired by Harlan’s work — trust graphs, node sculptures, and mycelial networks — in the luminous warm palette rather than the usual cold particle-web. Scroll through; each animates when it’s on screen. My favourite is marked.

1Mycelium

My favourite. A living force-graph: warm nodes and filaments that breathe, drift, and slowly reorganise. The clearest expression of trust graphs and mycelial networks, and it leaves a calm area on the left for the headline.

2Sculpture

A slowly rotating node-sculpture — thousands of points of warm light on an organic sphere, laced with faint links. Evokes “node sculptures created by AI while you sleep.” The most jewel-like of the three.

3Flow field

Points streaming along a slow curl of light, like data finding its channels. The most atmospheric and painterly; less overtly a “graph,” more a field of emergence.

Self-contained: vendored three.js, no external requests. Honours reduced-motion (renders a still frame).
