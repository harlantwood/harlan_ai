# Project Luminous — image generation prompts

## 1. Social preview card

- **Intended filename:** `sol/images/harlan-og.jpg`
- **Aspect ratio / output size:** 1.91:1, 1200 × 630 px
- **Placement:** Open Graph and Twitter/X preview image for `https://ha.rlan.ai/sol/`; not shown
  in the page body. After generating and checking the text, add absolute `og:image` and
  `twitter:image` metadata pointing to `https://ha.rlan.ai/sol/images/harlan-og.jpg`.
- **Prompt:** Create a finished editorial social preview card for software architect and AI
  transformation consultant Harlan T Wood. The composition feels like warm ivory paper lit from
  behind during the first minutes after sunrise. A single enormous amber solar disc is cropped by
  the right edge and crossed by one hairline deep-teal horizon; there are faint dawn-rose and pale
  gold halations, generous empty space, subtle paper grain, and no other illustration. On the left,
  typeset exactly two pieces of text: small uppercase sans-serif `HARLAN T WOOD`, then a very large
  humanist transitional serif headline on two or three lines, `Build what matters. Make AI yours.`
  Use deep mineral teal for all text with excellent contrast. The overall feeling is luminous,
  exact, calm, daring, and deeply human. It must not resemble a SaaS template or an AI startup.
  No neon, black background, circuits, brains, robots, stars, mandalas, sacred geometry, icons,
  logos, interface mockups, gradients inside the letters, extra words, or invented credentials.
  Keep all text inside a 72 px safe margin and ensure the punctuation and spelling are exact.
